# Field proof

Obtain the evidence that more implementation cannot manufacture. Read [working boundaries](../../references/working-contract.md) and [Veto's scope](../../references/veto-contract.md). A study plan is not permission to recruit, send, record, collect sensitive material, charge or admit a live file.


## Turn service contact into product improvement

The [Stripe-inspired loop](../../references/stripe-operating-system.md) uses hands-on help to learn, then tests whether the next intended user can complete the job without hidden rescue. Separate necessary teaching, avoidable friction, missing capability and intended professional work. Support contact earns a change when it exposes a transferable cause, not just when the response is faster.

Do not protect a founder's favored bet from contrary evidence. Preserve the first declared claim and recommendation, compare the actual outcome, and allow repair, narrowing or stopping. A cold agent walkthrough can prepare observation; it cannot replace an uncoached human attempt.

## Match evidence to the stage

Exploration may use a permissioned recent-file walkthrough to discover the task. Authorized learning needs bounded objective, data, people, resources and safeguards; manual help may be legitimate and must be counted. Live reliance needs real eligibility, procedure, qualified accepted ownership/coverage, supported inputs/contact/retention, terms, readiness, release approval, endpoint and recovery. Expansion needs repeated choice under actual terms and credible recurring delivery. Do not demand mature margins before discovery or call discovery permission live authority.

## Choose a discriminating observation

Follow the actual officer's job before and after the screen. Observe existing tools, qualified judgment, re-entry, interruptions, support and ordinary filing. Separate buyer preferences from operator/participant behavior. A missing source or absent qualified reviewer may be the constraint rather than interface quality.

Use [the field card](../../templates/field-card.md). Before a comparison, declare one claim: a named activity removed, material effort/interruptions reduced, or a supported control/continuity benefit. Name the competent baseline, practical threshold, safeguards, participant-burden limits and decision consequence. Do not invent a threshold from a pretty number or retrospectively rename reduction as removal.

Where only an internal prototype is available, test technical possibility and generate research questions. Simulated personas and agent walkthroughs do not establish comprehension, willingness to pay, actual source truth or market size.

## Observe without manufacturing success

For usability, give a realistic task without explaining the desired route. Watch entry, evidence inspection, correction, help, leaving and resumption, and ask the person what the consequential action did. If safety or accepted support requires intervention, give it and record it. Do not withhold needed help to protect a metric or coach until the user repeats the intended explanation.

Count all relevant eligible opportunities and attempts, including bypass, refusal, abandonment, exclusion and missing observations. Keep customer, participant and operator work visible. Separate recurring delivery, rescue, shadow validation, setup, reusable development and learning. Cash costs and time remain distinguishable; no conversion into invented ROI.

Use data minimization. Analytics should record necessary events and scoped identifiers, not wire details, original documents, secrets or private notes. Instrumentation and session replay require their own permitted purpose and configuration. No automatic provider connection is created here.

## Decide what was learned

Compare the original claim with observed results and limits. A failed comparison stays failed. An unexpected benefit is a new prospective hypothesis. A useful free file is not paid demand; one repeated choice is not PMF; absence of fraud in a small sample does not establish prevention.

Return the exact observation, denominator, assistance and uncertainty, plus the next earned allocation: deepen, repair, narrow, change route or stop. Preserve existing obligations when changing the future bet. Name what is unknown and the fastest permitted observation to resolve it; do not request another broad research report when the next evidence comes from a person using the product.
