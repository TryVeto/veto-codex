# Stripe-inspired edition: evidence and adaptations

Prepared 11 September 2026. This upgrade builds on Veto Stack 2026.9.1101 and the supplied materials. It is not an official Stripe plugin, a recovered internal handbook, or proof that copying practices causes Stripe-like outcomes. Veto's product boundaries and accepted design remain Veto's.

## The supplied basis

**F1 — How Stripe Ships, 91 pages.** The independent study distinguishes contemporary history, later recollection and practical synthesis. We retain its loop: real task → direct evidence → informed owner → bounded change → dependable use → new evidence. Relevant sections: first-use and support (chapters 3–7); autonomy and context (8–10); dependable changes and test environments (11–14); later review practices (19); causal limitations and stage-specific adaptation (21–25); compact working records (Appendix C). The study expressly rejects treating later rituals as first-year procedures. We do not turn its recommendations into documentary claims about Stripe.

**F2 — Supplied skill documents.** The nine distinct sources are preserved under [source-skills](source-skills/README.md), outside the discoverable skills directory. Duplicate filename variants were byte-deduplicated. [The input register](upgrade-inputs.json) records hashes and lineage. The shipping skill supplies the complete outcome loop; leadership and feature-team skills supply ownership and bounded delegation; designer/frontend skills supply task-first craft; sandbox/local-development skills supply safe rehearsal and reproducible state; backend/integration skills supply state, recovery and actual external boundaries. These are source material, not nine additional operating systems to activate.

**F3 — Product Foundations v10 and inherited Veto material.** Retain receiving preparation, scoped office judgment, accepted follow-through and normal-file handoff; distinct buyer funding; source/version/actor continuity; independent concerns and permitted repair; truthful saving, assistance and completion. Newer analyses remain proposals unless adopted. The first office's demand, current application behavior, operating readiness and provider access are not established by these documents.

**F4 — Two supplied Head-of-Engineering screenshots.** They record feedback and an agent's account of work: seller-link priority, confusing role/account labels, visible active navigation, sandbox sample inputs, and eyebrow removal. They do not expose all embedded product screens at readable resolution or prove current bugs. The upgrade converts these into conditional review examples and regression prompts—not claims that a new product audit reproduced them.

## What this release adds, rather than attributes to Stripe

The separately pinned review request, strict materials manifest, neutral-first ZIP option, scenario/viewport inventory, screenshot-feedback calibration cases, disagreement protocol and role-specific agent instructions are Veto designs. The optional pack builder organizes supplied evidence; it neither captures it nor guarantees its truth. No source demonstrates that this exact arrangement outperforms the prior plugin. [Evaluation is the next proof](../evals/JUDGMENT-CALIBRATION.md).

## Sources are not runtime dependencies

Agents read the task's relevant reference, not this research on every run. The former release's research and verification are retained as history and labeled by their edition; this edition's actual checks are in [VERIFICATION](../VERIFICATION.md). Public sources were consulted for mechanisms and current host details. No external source can expand Veto's mandate.
