# First feature and surfaces: recommendations, not adopted architecture

Basis: supplied First Feature, Product Surfaces and Integrations analyses dated 11 September 2026. See [source map](../research/SOURCE-MAP.md). These are product recommendations, not a current code audit, demand finding or expansion of operating permissions.

## Leading first feature

A **prepared receiving-instruction review**: start from the office's permitted existing material; prepare the question and its evidence; obtain only necessary contribution; carry the follow-up Veto actually accepted; retain the qualified person's scoped act against its basis; return the correct Review Record into the normal closing file.

“Prepare this review” does not mean inventing instructions, approving a wire or promising arrival of money. The ordinary sufficient case is the principal design test. It should not need an invented question, new signature or a manager route to demonstrate diligence.

One eligible seller's domestic USD receiving-instruction job is the proposed first scope, within confirmed terms. Entity/trust/estate/changed-destination diagnostic examples do not become live eligible because a prototype supports them. Preserve existing buyer-funding obligations; do not delete them to sharpen this experiment.

The first permissioned walkthrough can defeat this recommendation. If the actual constraint is initial source access, expert review capacity, institutional execution or financial protection, select an appropriate new bounded undertaking through the real decision owner. Do not expand software to conceal that preparation cannot solve the constraint.

## Three substantive customer surfaces

| Surface | Its earned job | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Office file with focused review | Reconstruct context once; inspect basis; make a scoped act; retrieve current/historical result. | A mandatory dashboard before an already known task. |
| Participant task | Supply, correct or acknowledge a particular contribution with understandable effect and useful help. | Requiring a continuing workspace, profile or internal-office vocabulary for a one-time task. |
| Returned Review Record | Put the exact issued result where the next authorized colleague normally works. | Treating export success or storage in Veto's bucket as promised filing. |

Supporting families are **entry/notifications/help**, **internal service desk**, and **office controls**. These six families are not six apps, six sidebar destinations or six parallel engineering projects. A service desk may use an existing suitable tool; controls may initially be assisted. Internal durable state does not compel a customer console.

## Location must earn its burden

Linked, embedded, structured exchange and continuing workspace are alternatives, not a universal ranking. A focused hosted task can beat an expensive integration. A continuing workspace can win when repeated cross-file prioritization, coverage and comparison genuinely improve. Email-only can be too forgetful. An AI chat can help variable inquiry but should not hide stable current state or blur commitment.

For a new surface, identify person/trigger, act improved, strongest existing route, incremental customer burden, and what becomes materially worse without the surface. First choose responsibility, then interaction, then location, then presentation. A database entity does not automatically deserve navigation.

Two distinct comparisons: competent office route versus Veto preparation-and-return tests the service; preparation-and-return versus a continuing workspace tests the incremental surface. Hold necessary service quality reasonably comparable, count its production cost, and do not build three complete products just to compare them.

## Dependency sequence, not reset

Join the current contribution and exact office retrieval; make the review inspectable, disagreeable and recoverable; establish usable record return. Keep working components and test the earliest unsatisfied join before prescribing repairs. A tiny fix may close the highest-value gap. An honest integrated slice is more informative than a broader collection of convincing screens.

## Updated surface contract from the supplied surface skill

Choose **responsibility → interaction → location → presentation**. Three work surfaces are an office file with focused review, the participant's limited task and the Review Record/handoff. Three supports are entry/notifications/help, internal service operations and restricted office controls. These are six families, not six apps or mandatory navigation items.

Known tasks should open directly while preserving context through required authentication. A file can hold several distinct jobs. Add a cross-file Work surface only for an actual prioritization or coverage job. Do not expose an internal primitive as a module merely because it exists. Waiting can need an owner and checkpoint, not a manufactured primary action.

For each surface, specify the actual actor/audience, file/request/question/basis, permitted act, durable effect, receipt, next owner and usable endpoint. Keep work lists, counts and current wrappers consistent with those facts without exposing the same private material to everyone. Review keeps the question beside decisive evidence and full originals reachable. Ordinary sufficient work follows prepare → required review → deliver; requests and assistance are conditional, not a scripted obstacle course.

Compare the competent obtainable office arrangement (O) with preparation-and-return (R). Compare R with added workspace (W) only when the additional attendance is the question. Keep safeguards and count actual preparation, support, maintenance and reconciliation in each. W versus O does not isolate workspace value. Do not build all three alternatives or erase useful current screens for the experiment.
