# Financial phone experience — conditional guidance

Load only when a selected task includes phone-assisted financial intake or relevant copy. This is adapted from the supplied `financial-phone-experience` skill, not a new feature mandate, legal opinion or permission to call, record, deploy, access an account or move money.

**Phone-assisted, not phone-exposed.** Conversation explains and helps. An assessed capture mechanism handles sensitive entry. Consequential authorization is a separate act. End with an accurate retrievable result and owned next step. Keep a usable non-smartphone alternative where the actual service supports it; a human helper is not automatically a safe capture boundary.

For each field identify purpose, necessity, audience, capture route, retention basis and correction/refusal treatment. Distinguish identification, authentication, account/control evidence, authority, rail suitability and authorization of this particular act. A suffix aids recognition; it is not unique identity or ownership proof.

Apply the source's six tests: **Legitimacy, Necessity, Comprehension, Control, Containment and Closure**. They are design lenses, not a validated numerical scale. Make caution compatible with completion: a person may end an unverified call, contact the organization through an independently established route, and resume the same task. Caller ID, a familiar voice or another link from the caller does not make that contact independent.

Never ask someone to dictate bank passwords, recovery secrets or bank sign-in codes to an assistant. Do not claim the assistant cannot hear or retain information unless the complete capture chain supports it. Keypad entry, masking or later redaction alone does not establish containment. Assess telephony, audio, transcription, model input, recorders, logs, analytics, transfers and storage at the layers the claim requires.

Keep required permissions for contact, recording/processing, collection, sharing and the consequential act distinct under the applicable procedure. Refresh current provider and jurisdiction-specific requirements before real implementation or reliance; this reference cannot settle them. Automation disclosure does not establish prior permission to place a call.

A text rehearsal proves neither audio behavior nor data containment. A failed or unknown capture boundary blocks that route; use only an assessed permitted alternative rather than ordinary dictation for conversion's sake. Preserve partial work and the original question. Return to [integration design](../skills/integration-design/SKILL.md) or the selected product task without creating a separate phone program.
