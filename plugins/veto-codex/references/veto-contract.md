## Five principles in behavior

**Explainability:** show the actual question beside its decisive source. Preserve full originals and contrary evidence; distinguish participant assertion, provider observation, AI interpretation and office determination. No favorable determination preselected. Another summary of the same source is not independent corroboration.

**Simplicity:** the person knows what this concerns, their next permitted act, its consequence, and where their part ends. Sufficient material should not acquire invented questions, checks, signatures, profiles, tours or manager approvals. The participant's task is smaller than the office workflow. Keep required personal acts and access safeguards.

**Collaboration:** contribution, correction, help, returned question, review and handoff concern the same file/request/instruction and relevant question editions. Roles retain their audiences. Assignment is not accepted coverage or a professional decision. An internal help note is not assistance delivered to the participant. The immediate obligation after accepted help belongs to its real helper, not a fictitious waiting-on-seller state.

**Reliability:** acknowledged work survives the promised interruptions. Draft, committed act, notice, filing and receipt stay separate. Reconcile an unknown save before a consequential retry; retry failed delivery without repeating a saved act. Recheck authority and the complete relevant basis at commitment. Preserve immutable originals/history and private drafts. Material change renews affected work; unrelated change does not erase everything.

**Trustworthiness:** independent concerns remain effective until each is resolved. A restriction may allow its explicit remedy; a pause or no-contact constraint still stops affected action. New material does not lift a pause. Rejecting a replacement cannot resurrect a withdrawn original. Finish at the agreed destination for the correct issued edition; do not add a ceremonial acceptance click when adequate destination evidence suffices. Current queues/counts/next actions must agree with that endpoint. Historical completion and later owned work remain distinct under the actual observation and transfer terms.

## Design and evidence

Preserve the accepted visual direction, readable type, compact useful structure, source-adjacent review and narrow participant task. **No decorative eyebrows, kickers or overlines above headings.** Keep functional form labels, meaningful status and required disclosures. Remove repetition before shrinking text. A reference teaches a relationship, not imported capabilities or a new brand. Apply the project WCAG 2.2 AA target without certifying it from a screenshot or automated scan.

Seller receiving, buyer acknowledgment, buyer-reported sending, bank observation and office receipt have different meanings. Test buyer regressions when shared mechanisms change; do not launch a second expansion program to prove the seller route.

The first learning focus is one eligible office, one permissioned file and one predeclared benefit. Receiving-preparation-and-return is a leading hypothesis; a recent-file walkthrough may select a different task. Declare removal versus reduction versus control/continuity before measuring against the competent obtainable alternative. Count participant burden, Veto handling and founder rescue, not just screen time. Exploration, authorized learning, live admission and commercial expansion require different evidence; a costly learning file is not automatically bad, and free use is not paid demand.
