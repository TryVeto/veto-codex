# One Real Wire: six continuity contracts

Source-derived requirements from Product Foundations v10 and the supplied One Real Wire skill/analyses. Apply by selected route dependency; this is not a list of newly observed bugs or permission to rebuild the platform. [Source map](../research/SOURCE-MAP.md).

## 1. Connected identity

Preserve file, request, instruction edition, actual actor, and relevant question/source references through entry, contribution, correction, help, review and handoff. Several independent questions on one file stay independent. Cross-role lists derive from shared facts rather than matching mock records.

Verification: submit from the participant's permitted session, reload both sessions, retrieve the exact contribution in the intended officer file. Reject wrong actor/request/file and unauthorized disclosure. Start from material already sufficient; unnecessary re-entry and extra collection are failures too.

## 2. Saved work and current authority

Keep original sources separate from editable proposals. Bind committed decisions to the relevant complete material basis, procedure and required acts, and recheck permission/currentness when committing. Avoid last-write-wins authority. New material is not automatically more authoritative. Material changes renew affected work without erasing history; an unrelated filename correction need not invalidate everything.

Draft, committed act, notice, issuance, delivery and external observation are separate. Pre-commit storage failure cannot show durable success. A lost post-commit response needs reconciliation of the original operation before another consequential act. Retrying filing must not recommit a review. Every editable value either persists as represented or yields an explicit failure.

A rejected replacement cannot revive withdrawn or compromised original instructions. Independent concerns keep independent identity and effect; resolving B must leave unresolved A effective in either resolution order. Preserve their existing data during migrations. A decision hold may allow expressly permitted preparation/help; pause/no-contact constraints still block their affected actions. Incoming material does not resume paused work.

## 3. One participant contribution

Show recognizable office, safe context, requested contribution, effect and a usable help route. Brand recognition is not authentication. A phone number found only in disputed material does not independently corroborate that material.

Support required full-detail inspection, correction, Back, leave/resume and a durable receipt on the same task. Preserve identifier strings/leading zeros. Suffix display and formatting validation do not prove full correctness, ownership or authority. Save progress only within actual privacy/retention rules, not an unprotected permanent browser cache.

The receipt says what was submitted and who acts next. It does not imply payment approval or execution. Preserve the actual person's required acts; a helper's involvement does not turn them into the participant.

## 4. A review that can disagree

Put compact file context, the actual question, material changes, relevant original passage/location and permitted disposition together. Keep full sources and omitted/contrary material accessible. AI selection can be wrong even when quotations are exact. Do not preselect a favorable determination or infer missing account digits, assent, authority or professional conclusions.

Keep participant assertions, provider observations, AI preparation and professional decisions distinct. Private drafts, shared notes, participant communications, questions and committed decisions have different audiences. Opening, assigning or reading is not deciding. Cover requires actual eligibility, availability and accepted responsibility; prior responsibility continues until the agreed transfer condition.

## 5. Help and pursuit that reach the person

Once help is accepted, its owner has the immediate obligation even when the participant still owes a contribution. Retain obstacle, progress, permitted channel and actual next checkpoint. An internal “help provided” note is not delivery. Offer a usable permitted alternative, not the same failed sequence under another label.

A returned question keeps identity, edition, reason, requested material, owner and checkpoint. Distinguish received, technically usable, prepared relevance and qualified substantive sufficiency. An auto-reply or unrelated attachment does not resolve it. Preserve useful partial work; explicitly reconcile a reply to an older question edition.

Accepted ongoing pursuit needs a real authorized runner or staffed owner beyond the tab. Recheck state/permission/audience/stop conditions before events or timers act. A supported obstacle is an endpoint only under the accepted offer and actual transfer rule, not because the implementation became inconvenient.

## 6. The agreed normal-file endpoint

Issue a canonical Review Record from acts actually performed. Screen, PDF, print and structured representations must agree on the issued edition. Establish the accepted destination event or required recipient act. No ceremonial click if the destination already supplies adequate evidence; no deletion of a required recipient check merely for convenience.

Keep filing failure owned; retry the same issued artifact without repeating the office decision. A covering authorized colleague retrieves the correct result normally. Active queues, counts, summaries and next acts agree with receiving-service completion, not an unrelated payment event.

A bank event, buyer report and receipt of funds require their own source, event/report times and limits. Unknown is not confirmed and not necessarily absent. Historical handoff stays fixed while late reports create linked new owned work under actual observation duration, transfer and renewed-work terms. Do not invent perpetual monitoring, prices or ability to stop the bank. The outage fallback must not require a fresh export from the unavailable service.

Keep buyer funding's separate direction, receiving-profile basis, personal acts and completion meanings. Shared changes require its own affected regression, not credit from the seller test.
