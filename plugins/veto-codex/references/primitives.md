# Veto primitives: the distinctions that must survive simplification

Adapted from the newly supplied `veto-primitives` skill. Its nine-family model is a recommendation, not an adopted database schema or proof of a deployed capability. Preserve sound existing implementation and actual customer terms. The office decides; its bank executes. Buyer funding remains a separate accepted journey.

**Job is the unit of accountability; File can remain the entry point.** Give a concept a distinct identity when it changes independently, crosses authority or audience, requires history, or must be addressed after interruption. Otherwise a typed value or existing record can be enough. Nine families do not mandate nine tables, services, agents or screens.

| Family | Distinction it preserves |
|---|---|
| File | Office-scoped transaction context, capable of containing several jobs. |
| Job | Accepted service, procedure/scope, owner, permissions, duration and endpoint. |
| Party | Identity, representation, contact provenance, participation and access are not one fact. |
| Instruction | Stable identity and immutable submitted editions; separate editable drafts. |
| Evidence | Retained sources, attributed observations and interpretations remain distinguishable. |
| Question | Exact unresolved proposition, basis and edition; receipt is not its resolution. |
| Obligation | Accepted operating work, owner, checkpoint, discharge and transfer condition. |
| Decision | An authorized actor's typed determination against the complete inspected basis. |
| Result | Issued edition, attempts and agreed endpoint evidence; export is not delivery. |

Before a consequential change, specify **actor/authority → exact object/basis → act → durable effect → receipt → remaining owner → failure/repair**. Test refusal and legitimate continuation. Preserve independent concerns, exact request/edition identity, source bytes and provenance, and current authorization at commitment.

Editing a proposal must not edit its evidence. A changed basis may reopen affected review; reject an unsupported carry-forward. Rejecting a replacement does not revive withdrawn instructions. Resolving one question does not clear another. A hold permits its authorized remedy while keeping unrelated constraints effective.

A missing response is not proof of failure. Reconcile the original operation before retrying a consequential act; recover a failed notice independently of a saved decision. Real atomicity and authorization require actual enforcement and tests, not UI copy.

Receiving completion concerns the accepted endpoint. Preserve issued history; current applicability and later owned work can change without rewriting that history. Retain duration, observation and late-report duties under actual terms. Do not invent perpetual monitoring, an owner, a provider or payment authority to complete the model.

Apply only the changed dependency path. Do not rename the entire application to match this vocabulary. Use [acceptance cases](acceptance-cases.md) for candidate-specific checks and [the source map](../research/WORK-CONTROL-SOURCE-MAP.md) for provenance.
