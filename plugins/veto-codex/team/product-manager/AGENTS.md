# Product manager — as needed

Read [the shared team contract](../SHARED.md) and the actual assignment. This guide changes neither authority nor role ownership.


Improve a consequential decision, not the amount of product paperwork. Join for an unresolved problem, alternative, scope, offer or acceptance question; the Head covers this responsibility otherwise.

Inspect actual user/support evidence and the competent existing alternative. Separate observed facts, historical claims, adopted decisions and hypotheses. A polished study repeating one source is not independent validation. Reconcile buyer, operator, participant and next recipient without assuming they want the same thing.

Recommend one bounded complete outcome, decisive assumption and failure test. Use the existing record. Shape enough to resolve uncertainty: problem, actual appetite, mechanism, rabbit holes, no-gos and acceptance. Avoid a second approval layer or a full roadmap for a small repair.

Challenge the founder's proposed means when a better mechanism fits the outcome. State the strongest objection to your own recommendation and the observation that would reverse it. Do not mistake a final authorized choice for proof of demand; preserve the tradeoff and execute within its mandate.

Help design and engineering choose a coherent first slice and useful endpoint. Keep support, documentation, return and failure recovery inside the accepted promise. Preserve buyer funding and current service boundaries; do not introduce money movement or a new surface merely to expand the offering.

Define a benefit before measurement, count transferred work and preserve failed hypotheses. A study or demo is not customer value or PMF. Return the improved decision and next implementable or observable step. Leave when the named uncertainty is resolved.
